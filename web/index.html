StashDB Tagger for Pronoisseur

Match the videos in your Pronoisseur library against StashDB and write the results back through its Local API. Files are identified by the fingerprints Pronoisseur has worked out. If a folder name carries a StashDB scene id, as Whisparr's do, that is a shortcut, and the id is still checked against the file. Nothing is written until you have seen the diff.

    Reads your untagged videos from Pronoisseur. Where a folder name has a StashDB scene id, fetches that scene and checks it against the video's fingerprint. Nothing is written.

    Sends each remaining video's fingerprints to StashDB, 40 at a time. Nothing is written.

    Writes only the ticked videos to Pronoisseur. StashDB is only read.

    How this works
    1. Each video sits beside the StashDB scene it matched: a frame from your file next to the StashDB cover. If they are the same video, it is the right match.
    2. Matched The tool found this video's StashDB scene, and each card says how. The fingerprints it looks up come from Pronoisseur; nothing is hashed here. It is ticked, ready to tag. Untick any match that's wrong.
    3. Choose a match StashDB has more than one scene that could be this video, or one that is not close enough to be sure. Use this match on the right one, or None of these match. Recommended marks the best fit, and Use recommended takes it for you, but only where its length matches your file. A video you choose moves to Matched, ticked.
    4. No match StashDB has no scene with this file's fingerprint. Search for it by name.
    5. Pending hashing Pronoisseur has no perceptual fingerprint for this video yet, and its exact one found nothing on StashDB. Search for it by name, or scan and identify again once Pronoisseur has fingerprinted it.
    6. Tag writes the match of each ticked video the list is showing into Pronoisseur: title, date, studio, models and categories, and a link to the StashDB scene. The tabs and the filter narrow both Tag and Use recommended, exactly as they narrow the list. Nothing is removed, and your video files are never opened or changed.
